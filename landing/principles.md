# Principles

The beliefs every Silicon landing page follows. Each one is earned in a real round, not
written in advance, and each carries the reason it exists and the decision it came from.

Format:

```
## <Principle, as an instruction>
<One or two sentences on what it means in practice.>
Because: <the reason>. From: <decisions.md line / date>.
```

Keep this list short. If a principle never changes a design decision, delete it.

---

## Layer it: a background for personality, content on top
Every hero has two layers. The base is an abstract background: it gives the page its
character and stays quiet wherever text sits. The content is text plus one illustration
that supports it. Type stays calm; the layers carry the personality.
Because: all three R2 directions put type and cards on a flat page and none was good
enough. From: decisions.md, 2026-10-18 (R3).

## Colour is the brand's, not the app's
Every app uses brand blue, and only brand blue, for its backdrop, product name and primary button. Apps are
told apart by their illustration and one distinction device (to be chosen in R7), never
by colour.
Because: per-app hue dilutes the brand, and a family of app colours needs its own
careful work. From: decisions.md, 2026-10-18 (R7).

## One nav, owned by the company
Every Silicon site uses the TOS nav unchanged. Apps don't add a second bar; anything
audience-specific belongs in the page.
Because: R4 and R5's second bars duplicated links (Docs, Developers) and split one
purpose across three entries. From: decisions.md, 2026-10-18 (R6).
